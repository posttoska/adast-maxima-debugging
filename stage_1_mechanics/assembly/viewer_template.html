Coordinates, classes and abbreviations
Units: mm (millimetres). Axes: X along the knife, + to the operator's right; Y + from the cutting line toward the backgauge (the rear); Z up from the floor. Origin: middle of the cutting width, on the cutting line, at floor level. The model stands at rest: knife up, clamp up, backgauge at the rear. Device anchors are named <kind>.<designation in the electrical legend of wiring set 568-70.9001>, for example sensor.SQ1, valve.Y11, brake.Y14, motor.M1; foot.* and body.* anchors are not devices.
