## Files and how to run
All scripts take their paths from `__file__`; run them with `python` (3.15, numpy, trimesh, manifold3d, Pillow); nothing else is needed.
- `python R/parts/clamp/clamp.py [--spec <clamp.md>] [--out <dir>]` builds every body from the model table below and the anchors from the anchors table (no other numbers in the script). Writes `R/out/clamp.glb` (one node `clamp.<body>` per row), `R/out/clamp.stl` (all bodies), `R/out/clamp_anchors.json` (anchors, groups, per-body bounds and volumes). Prints one line per body and the part bounds; exit 0.
- `python R/parts/clamp/check_spec.py [--out <dir>]` reads the spec, the clamp outputs and the delivered frame, knife and backgauge files in R/out. Prints one PASS/FAIL line per check (section 04 checks 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11; joints; S28 and S30 places; hydraulic fit; pedal; envelopes; source numbers read back from the model rows: HC, P11, P7, HL; hoses OZ4, OZ6, OZ12, OZ14 by nearest faces; F101 places of the panel and OV2; stale outputs: the JSON anchors equal the spec's anchors table; prose sizes and strokes of the component rows equal the model table; r4: prose positions of the component rows equal the model table, device anchors lie on their bodies, S28 and S30 at their F101 readings, each envelope touches what its `touches=` names and the linkage chains are closed, the data dwell equals the winner of conflict 11; r5: no body above the overall height 1535 (P7), the rack meshes with gear 8 on its −X side over the whole stroke (F71), gear 8 and Y14 at F71's pinion reading, M3 gives OA1's outer size and the piston and rod lengths with errors, the knob length and its error cover PH150610's reading, and a one-sided X range whose mirror is another solid of the same body must be written ±a..±b; r6: gear 8 turns with the rack (its mesh point moves with the beam) by the stroke's turn about its own centre, with one axis in the anchor, the groups line and the JSON groups (tester 2 r3), and the mutant count of the line below equals the mutation test's), then `ALL PASS` and exit 0, or `FAIL n` and exit 1.
- `python R/parts/clamp/mutation_test.py` makes 41 broken copies of the spec (body moved, finger in a backgauge finger, tank into the knife space, anchor dropped, bbox drift, motor.M1 doubled, passage dropped, pedal into the corridor, S28 off the plate, S30 low, bore Ø90, rod Ø40, knob 3 Ø60, tube short, pedal 200 long, gauge Ø100, panel back at X 1127, OV2 back at X 420, an anchor changed without a rebuild, prose lift 150, prose bore Ø90; r4: gauge position prose X 1127, OV2 position prose X 340..500, gauge anchor off its body, pedal-link rise short of OV2, data dwell back to 0-3 s; r5: rack top back at 1625, rack top at F71's head 1490, rack on gear 8's +X side, Y14 back at Z 1475, F71 reading changed, guides prose one-sided (critic g6's survivor), knob 3 back to 25 long, M3 without OA1's outer error, knob length error ±10; r6: gear 8 about +Y against the rack (the r5 fault tester 2 found), its range negative, the groups line back at +Y, a range short of the stroke's turn, the pivot off the gear's centre, this line's count back at 26), rebuilds each into `R/parts/clamp/mutants/<name>/out` (overwritten, never deleted) and runs check_spec on it. Expected: every mutant gives at least one FAIL; prints `MUTANTS caught 41/41` and exit 0.
- `python R/parts/clamp/render_views.py` writes `R/parts/clamp/renders/clamp_views.png`: front, side and top panels at rest and with the clamp down 130, frame, knife and backgauge bodies as a light grey background, envelopes as yellow outlines.
