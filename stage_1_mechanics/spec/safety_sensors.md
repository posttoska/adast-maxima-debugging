# Safety devices and sensors (thread 6): one body for each device of section 10

Adast Maxima MS 115 PS, serial 578.0009. Millimetres; machine coordinates of section 05 (X along the knife (нож, P46 "фиксации ножа"), + to the operator's right; Y + from the cutting line to the rear; Z up, Z 0 floor); at rest = knife up, clamp (прижим, P46 "тормоз прижима") up. Classes: D drawing, T table or text, P photo proportion, E estimated, W web. OM = operating manual MS 80/115 PS, DS; the page keys (P44, F62 ...) are resolved in the Sources table.

Terms at first use: English first, then in brackets the Russian (RU) of the OM or the Czech (CZ) of the wiring set, quoted as printed, with its page: backgauge (седло, P46 "положения седла"; also затл, P46 "датчик положения затла"); button (кнопка, P44 "кнопки пуска реза"); emergency stop button, short E-stop (кнопка аварийной остановки, P44); head of a button or switch (головка, P44); sensor (датчик, P44 "датчики положения ножа", "датчики фиксации ножа"); switch (выключатель, P44, P46); limit switch, the kind of the Telemecanique XCK-A switches S5, S19, S28, S30 and of backgauge S24, S27 (концевой выключатель, P41 for S30; CZ koncový spínač, W568 for S5); motor (двигатель, P44 "главный двигатель привода ножа и гидравлики", "двигатель привода затла"); blower (воздуходувка, P44); electromagnet, which also names the valves Y3 and Y11 (электромагнит, P46); clutch (муфта, P46 "электромагнитная муфта реза"); brake (тормоз, P46 "тормоз прижима"); encoder BR40 (P46 prints "датчик положения затла", backgauge position sensor); light curtain (предохранительная диафрагма, F31; CZ "světelné clony", CERT); electrical cabinet (электрораспределитель, packing list item 2.8 as spec/housing.md quotes it). The Inventory gives each device its full printed name.

## How this part is built
- Every device of section 10 already has its anchor, published once by its host part (frame, knife, clamp, backgauge, housing): check 10 counts each designation once over all `out/*_anchors.json`, so this part publishes no device anchor again. Its anchors are named `body.<designation>`, one per body, at the body's actuation point (push face, plunger or roller tip, sensing face), with the host anchor's name and the offset from it.
- A body's solid is written relative to its host's device anchor, which `safety_sensors.py` reads at run time from `out/<host>_anchors.json`; a moved host anchor moves the body. The numbers below are offsets from that anchor (u, v, w along X, Y, Z).
- Ten devices already have a body in their host part (Inventory, column "body"); they get no second body. 18 bodies are built here. FF1 is not fitted: no body (Inventory, conflict 1).
- Bodies on a face (buttons, E-stop, cabinet switches) are the parts that stand in front of the face. What sits behind the face (adapter, contact block, switch body) is inside the host, which the host models as a solid (requirement R4: simple solids, no fasteners): not modelled, nothing invented.
- Body sizes: no file of this machine prints a device's size. Where a photo of this machine shows the device, its proportions give the size (P, ±15 %); otherwise the size is estimated (E) from the type printed in the device list (P44, P46), or it is the room the host model leaves at the anchor (E, said so in the row). The type sizes come from the modeller's memory of the makers' catalogues, not from any file here, and were not checked against a catalogue: the Honeywell BZ basic switch 49 x 25 x 18 (S12, S13, S14); the BACO V22 heads of S10, S11 (Ø30, 10 proud) and the S1 mushroom (Ø40). The Telemecanique XCK-A box 31 x 30 x 95 (S5, S19, S28 width, S30) is the size spec/backgauge.md gives its XCK-A 102 switches S24, S27, also E there ("no data sheet among the sources"). All are class E with a measure row. No web page was read for them (order 1, question 2: web only for Adast Maxima pages).
- Orientation, that is which way a body lies and which way its actuator faces (dir= in the Bodies table), has three grounds. Forced by the host's face: the heads S10, S11, S1, Q1, Q2, S2, S4 face out of their face (-Y); the face and its place are the host's (frame M14, housing M3 and M12). Facing forced by a modelled target, lay of the box chosen (E): SQ1, SQ2 (+X, the flag on knife.y01_tail), S5 (+X, the cover wall), S30 (-Y, the clamp bar; box laid along Y), S28 (-Z, the false plate; box upright). Chosen with nothing to go by (E): S12, S13, S14 (-Y), S19 (+Z), and the lay of M3 (long side along X) and Y3 (long side along Z), which have no actuator. Gaps and the measure rows SS2-SS8 carry the chosen ones.
- Housing state: the bodies on housing anchors (S1, Q1, Q2, S2, S4) and S5 (pressed by housing.belt_pulley_cover) stand on out/housing.glb and out/housing_anchors.json of 12:22:34 (housing r6, which moved button.S1 from X 785 to 773; the S1 body followed). The housing thread closed at 12:50:18 (THREAD_CLOSED_actor_t5) on those same outputs. Rebuilt on them at 12:54:59 with this spec's round 3 text: byte-equal to the round 3 outputs of 12:23:30, check_spec.py ALL PASS. Nothing changed. Round 4 (this text) rebuilt at 12:56:55: GLB and STL byte-equal again; the anchors file differs only in the class of body.S10, body.S11 and body.M3 (now E), in body.Y3's target text and in the motion-group text, which the script now writes from the spec's motion cells.
- One overlap is excused, and only one: S28 x clamp.front_block, inside the clamp's zone X -510..-470, Y 2..24, Z 1021..1101 (clamp open item 5: the beam is modelled solid, the switch sits in a pocket of it). S28 moves with the beam, so it is checked against the beam's bodies at rest and, swept over the stroke, against everything else.
- Swept host motions and keep-out boxes (hosts.py: SWEEPS, KEEPOUT) count as occupied: a body may not stand where a moving host part passes during its whole stroke.

## Bodies
Solid grammar, offsets from the host anchor: `box x=u0..u1 y=v0..v1 z=w0..w1`; `cyl ax=<axis> d=<diameter> s=<from>..<to>` along the axis, centred on the anchor in the other two; `+` joins solids. Actuator: `at=u,v,w dir=<facing>; <target>`. Error: size and place of the body relative to its host anchor; the host anchor's own error is added in the anchors file (host_error_mm).
